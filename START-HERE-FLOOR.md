# Floor — start here (no GitHub)

You do **not** need GitHub, git, or a developer laptop setup.

## Steps

1. **Unzip** `sales-generator-for-floor.zip` (double-click on your Mac).
2. Open the unzipped folder in **Claude Cowork** or **Claude Code** (Attach / Open folder).
3. Paste this prompt:

> Pull today’s **Dutch** HubSpot tasks. Draft LinkedIn + email sequences to approve — do **not** send. Skip Belgian leads.

4. Review drafts → approve before anything is treated as sent.
5. Teach it anytime: `Remember this feedback: …`

## When a lead wants a demo

Do **not** let Claude auto-book a calendar.

1. Claude posts to **Slack** for you with: lead/company, the **conversation** (transcript or clear summary), why they’re interested, and links.
2. **You** manually book the meeting on **Ludwig’s calendar** and verify the invite.
3. After it’s booked, tell Claude (or update HubSpot) → **Demo Booked**, then later **Completed** / **Rescheduled** / **Cancelled**.
