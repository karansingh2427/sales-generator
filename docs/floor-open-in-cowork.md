# Floor — open in Claude Cowork (your computer)

Unzip on **your** Mac → open **that** folder in Cowork (HubSpot, Lemlist, Slack, Gmail connected).

Paste the **one setup message** from `START-HERE-FLOOR.md` once. It creates three automatic tasks (Mon–Fri, Amsterdam time):

- **08:00** — find new Dutch HubSpot leads (newest first, up to 2 months back), write their messages, check them, and add the good ones to Lemlist “Willow NL pilot”. Leads Claude is unsure about get ⚠️ and wait for you.
- **Every hour 08:00–18:00** — Slack you about every new reply, with a draft answer (nothing is sent without your OK).
- **08:50** — a short Slack update on how it’s going.

You only: handle ⚠️ leads, read “today’s texts” now and then, OK replies, and book Ludwig when someone wants a demo.
Emergency stop: type **“Stop the automatic outreach”**.

Your laptop needs to be on with Claude open for the tasks to run.
